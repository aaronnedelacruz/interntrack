Frequently Asked Questions

What is InternTrack?

InternTrack is a web-based internship management platform designed to help students monitor attendance, track internship hours, manage projects, and generate reports.

Can I edit my attendance records?

Yes. You can update or delete previously submitted attendance logs whenever corrections are needed.

Does InternTrack automatically calculate my remaining hours?

Yes. The system automatically updates your completed and remaining internship hours after every logged session.

Can I track multiple internship projects?

Absolutely. You can organize your work into multiple projects while monitoring the hours spent on each one.

Can I export my reports?

Yes. InternTrack allows you to generate and export professional internship reports for documentation and submission.

Is my data secure?

Your internship records are securely stored to help ensure your information remains safe and accessible whenever you need it.

About InternTrack

About InternTrack

InternTrack is a modern internship management platform designed to simplify internship monitoring for students.

Instead of manually recording attendance through spreadsheets or paper logs, InternTrack provides an organized digital workspace where users can log hours, manage projects, monitor progress, and view performance through interactive reports.

Our goal is to make internship tracking simple, efficient, and stress-free so students can focus more on learning and professional growth.

Our Mission

To provide students with a reliable, organized, and user-friendly platform for managing internship attendance, progress, and reporting.

Our Vision

To become a trusted digital companion for internship management by promoting productivity, accuracy, and transparency throughout every student's internship journey.
